Helper functions support transformations:

rotatePoint → rotates a 2D point around a center.

detectNailOrientation → finds angle of the nail polygon based on farthest point from centroid.

simpleNailMatching → simple 1:1 index mapping (design nail i → target nail i).

simpleNailAnalysis → analyzes size, aspect ratio, direction of a nail polygon.

calculateNailDirection → finds angle from centroid to farthest point (nail tip).

getSimpleCentroid → average of all points (polygon center).

getSimpleBounds → bounding box (min/max x/y, width, height).
